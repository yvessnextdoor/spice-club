01 / WHO WE ARE

Change begins
where we stand.

SPICE Club is the environmental and community initiative of St. Agnes School, Kharagpur.

We believe that a better world is built through everyday choices: caring for trees, reducing waste, sharing knowledge, helping others and inspiring our school community to take part.
